## 4. Base de datos (SQLite + Drizzle)

**5 tablas:** `vehicles`, `ecus`, `pid_definitions`, `pid_readings`, `diagnosis_sessions`

**Tipo genérico:** `DiagnosticsDb` en `db.ts` — para migrar a PostgreSQL solo se cambia este fichero.

**Seed data:** 22 PIDs en `seed-pids.ts`:
- 16 Mode 01 (RPM, coolant, speed, STFT, LTFT, MAF, throttle, fuel tank...)
- 1 Mode 09 (VIN — ASCII 17 chars)
- 4 Mode 22 Toyota Auris (TCU/ECM odometer, hybrid battery SOC/voltage)

**Scripts DB:**

## 9. Catálogo de PIDs (22 definiciones)

### Mode 01 — Standard SAE J1979 (16 PIDs)

| PID | Nombre | Fórmula | Bytes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 04 | Engine Load | `A*100/255` | 1 |
| 05 | Coolant Temp | `A-40` | 1 |
| 06 | STFT Bank 1 | `A*100/128-100` | 1 |
| 07 | LTFT Bank 1 | `A*100/128-100` | 1 |
| 0B | MAP | `A` | 1 |
| 0C | RPM | `(A*256+B)/4` | 2 |
| 0D | Speed | `A` | 1 |
| 0E | Timing Advance | `A/2-64` | 1 |
| 0F | Intake Air Temp | `A-40` | 1 |
| 10 | MAF Air Flow | `(A*256+B)/100` | 2 |
| 11 | Throttle | `A*100/255` | 1 |
| 2F | Fuel Tank | `A*100/255` | 1 |
| 31 | Distance Since Clear | `A*256+B` | 2 |
| 42 | Module Voltage | `(A*256+B)/1000` | 2 |
| 46 | Ambient Temp | `A-40` | 1 |
| 5C | Oil Temp | `A-40` | 1 |

### Mode 09 — Vehicle Info (1 PID)

| PID | Nombre | Fórmula | Bytes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 02 | VIN | `pidType: 'ascii'` | 17 |

### Mode 22 — Toyota Auris Hybrid (4 PIDs)

| PID | ECU | Nombre | Fórmula | Bytes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0300 | TCU (7E1) | TCU Odometer | `(A<<24\|B<<16\|C<<8\|D)/10` | 4 |
| 0400 | ECM (7E0) | ECM Odometer | `(A<<24\|B<<16\|C<<8\|D)/10` | 4 |
| 7A76 | ECM (7E0) | Hybrid Battery SOC | `A*0.5` | 1 |
| 7A53 | ECM (7E0) | Hybrid Battery Voltage | `(A*256+B)*0.01` | 2 |
